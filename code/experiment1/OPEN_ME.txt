EVOLUTION — STUDY MOCKUP

Double-click index.html to open the study in your browser.

If you received a ZIP, unzip the whole folder first. Keep the assets, css, js,
and vendor folders alongside index.html. No internet or software installation
is needed. A current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser is recommended.

Work through consent, familiarity, instructions, and the six randomized cases.
Every case includes a realistic generated picture. Both interacting species
appear in each of the two interacting-species pictures.

The two hard cases each ask for three possible natural-selection explanations.
Demographics and an optional feedback question come at the end.

Download responses as CSV or JSON on the completion screen if you want to keep
them. This mockup does not upload responses or issue payments. Closing or
refreshing the tab discards unsaved responses; refreshing starts a new order.
