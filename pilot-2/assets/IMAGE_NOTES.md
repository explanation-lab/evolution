# Images

All six PNGs in `images/` were generated with the built-in image_gen tool on October 11, 2026 for this mockup. David requested realistic generated pictures. No downloaded stock or Wikimedia pictures are used.

- `duck.png`: modern mallard, with webbed feet visible.
- `giraffe.png`: full giraffe with its long neck visible.
- `cheetah-gazelle.png`: neutral two-panel image of a cheetah and gazelle.
- `moth-bird.png`: neutral two-panel image of a moth on bark and insect-eating bird.
- `goat.png`: goat head with its horizontal pupil visible.
- `silver-ant.png`: macro image of a Saharan silver ant with silvery hairs.

These are photorealistic generated illustrations, not documentary photographs or evidence for the hypothetical histories in the study. The full prompt set is included at `../../../docs/image-prompts.json` in the handoff folder.
