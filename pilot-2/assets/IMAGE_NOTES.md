## Pilot 2 worked example: finch beaks

`images/finch-beaks.png` (1536 × 1024) was generated on October 15, 2026 with the built-in image_gen tool in new-image mode. It is an illustrative hypothetical population, not a record of a historical study. The three beak sizes support the variation step; the question and explanation address population change over generations.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a realistic natural-history photograph for an educational study about evolution. Wide landscape composition, approximately 3:2. Three adult brown ground finches from the same population sit side by side on a low dry branch, each in clear side profile facing right, at the same scale and with similar small bodies and plain brown plumage. Their beaks show plausible within-population variation: left bird has a relatively small shallow conical seed-cracking beak, middle bird has an intermediate conical beak, right bird has a visibly larger deeper thicker conical beak. Keep differences clear but biologically plausible. All three heads, eyes and full beaks must be unobstructed, separated and sharply in focus. Soft neutral dry scrub background out of focus, natural daylight, restrained realistic colors, documentary wildlife photography. No text, labels, arrows, diagrams, inset panels, watermarks, extra birds, exaggerated beaks or human objects. This is an illustrative hypothetical population, not an image of a named historical experiment.
