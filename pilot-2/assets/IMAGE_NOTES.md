### `images/barn-owl-ears.png`

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create a carefully drawn realistic natural-history educational illustration, landscape 3:2, of an adult American barn owl's head viewed straight from the front against a plain neutral light background. The two eyes and beak are level and symmetrical. On both sides of the head the feathers are gently parted in small clean windows to reveal the soft-tissue ear openings normally hidden behind the facial feathers. The owl's ANATOMICAL LEFT ear opening, on the VIEWER'S RIGHT, is distinctly HIGHER than its anatomical right ear opening, on the viewer's left. The ear openings sit lateral to and slightly behind the eyes. This is soft-tissue asymmetry; do not distort the skull or eye levels. Show realistic narrow ear openings with no human external ears, no horns, no ear tufts, no blood or injuries. Keep the normal recognizable heart-shaped white/tan barn-owl facial disk and detailed soft plumage. The reveal of the concealed ears must be visibly an anatomical illustration rather than a purported unaltered photograph. No prey, no sound waves, no functional explanation, no text or labels or watermarks. The offset ear positions should be easy to distinguish at small screen size.

### `images/lanternfish-light-organs.png`

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create a realistic natural-history illustration with photographic textures of one small deep-sea lanternfish, based on the general appearance of Diaphus watasei, in clear side profile facing left against a simple dark blue open-water background. Landscape 3:2 composition. Show the full slim silvery-brown fish, large round eye, ordinary modest jaws, fins and tail, with a clear row of small distinct pale blue luminous photophores along the ventral lower flank and underside. The purpose is simply to show the light-producing organs without revealing their function. No text, labels, arrows or watermark.

### `images/magnetic-bacteria.png`

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: scientific-educational. Create a clear realistic three-dimensional scientific cutaway illustration, landscape 3:2, of a single magnetotactic bacterium inspired by Magnetospirillum gryphiswaldense. Light neutral blue-gray background. A gently curved elongated spiral bacterial cell lies horizontally, with thin hairlike flagella extending from its ends. A clean schematic cutaway of its outer envelope reveals ONE straight-to-gently-curved ordered chain of about 15 small dark iron-mineral crystals along the cell's long axis. Each crystal is individually distinguishable; the continuous chain is the central visual feature. The cytoplasm is softly translucent pale beige, cell envelope pale blue gray, crystals dark charcoal, natural scientific rendering without dramatic neon. No nucleus, no mitochondria, no internal compass object, no horseshoe magnet, no Earth globe, no oxygen gradient, no food, no functional explanation. This should clearly be a magnified explanatory illustration, not claimed microscopy. No text, labels, arrows or watermark.

## Pilot 2 worked example: finch beaks

`images/finch-beaks.png` (1536 × 1024) was generated on October 15, 2026 with the built-in image_gen tool in new-image mode. It is an illustrative hypothetical population, not a record of a historical study. The three beak sizes support the variation step; the question and explanation address population change over generations.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a realistic natural-history photograph for an educational study about evolution. Wide landscape composition, approximately 3:2. Three adult brown ground finches from the same population sit side by side on a low dry branch, each in clear side profile facing right, at the same scale and with similar small bodies and plain brown plumage. Their beaks show plausible within-population variation: left bird has a relatively small shallow conical seed-cracking beak, middle bird has an intermediate conical beak, right bird has a visibly larger deeper thicker conical beak. Keep differences clear but biologically plausible. All three heads, eyes and full beaks must be unobstructed, separated and sharply in focus. Soft neutral dry scrub background out of focus, natural daylight, restrained realistic colors, documentary wildlife photography. No text, labels, arrows, diagrams, inset panels, watermarks, extra birds, exaggerated beaks or human objects. This is an illustrative hypothetical population, not an image of a named historical experiment.
