EVOLUTION - PILOT 2 REVIEW

Double-click index.html to open the study in your browser.

If you received a ZIP, unzip the whole folder first. Keep the assets, css, js,
and vendor folders alongside index.html. No internet or software installation
is needed. A current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser is recommended.

After consent, familiarity, and instructions, a short finch-beak worked example
appears. It is followed by ducks, gazelles and cheetahs together, moths and birds
together, goats, silver ants, barn owls, lanternfish, magnetic bacteria, and
finally giraffes. The worked example is no longer visible
while participants answer. The sequence is fixed.

There are nine response screens, each asking for one explanation. The original
pictures, styling, and controls are retained, with three new illustrated cases.
Demographics and required feedback come at the end;
enter "None" if you have no feedback.

Download responses as CSV or JSON on the completion screen if you want to keep
them. This review does not upload responses or issue payments. Closing or
refreshing the tab discards unsaved responses.
